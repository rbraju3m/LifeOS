# 4. User Personas

1. **"Tanvir" — Dhaka salaried professional, 29–38.** Android, mid-range phone, often on mobile data. Pays utility bills and has to handle NID corrections and passport renewal. Uses English at work and Bengali at home. Needs reliable reminders and a safe place for document scans. *Primary MVP persona.*
2. **"Nasrin" — household organiser, 35–50.** Runs school fees, doctor visits, relatives' birthdays and household chores. Prefers Bengali. Uses Facebook and WhatsApp heavily, not productivity apps. Needs a very simple UI and, later, shared family tasks. *Primary FamilyOS persona.*
3. **"Arif" — student / young professional, 19–26.** Tech-savvy, wants tasks and notes, likely free-tier forever, likely to spread the app by word of mouth. *Acquisition persona.*
4. **"Karim" — expat worker in UAE/KSA/Malaysia, 25–45.** Supports family in Bangladesh, lives in a different time zone, needs to see family obligations and documents remotely. *Drives the time zone and Family requirements.*
5. **"Abba/Amma" — elderly parent, 60+.** Low digital literacy. A family member sets up reminders for them. *Drives Phase 5 "managed member" and accessibility (large text, simple flows).*
